# Students Spending Habits Dashboard

This repository contains an interactive Power BI dashboard focused on analyzing students' spending habits, income patterns, financial aid, and lifestyle expenses.

The project transforms raw student financial data into actionable insights through data visualization and dashboard reporting.

---

# Project Overview

The purpose of this project is to:
- Analyze student spending behavior
- Understand financial aid impact on expenses
- Identify major spending categories
- Compare spending patterns across genders and school years
- Build an interactive business intelligence dashboard

# Key Insights

## Spending Categories
- Housing recorded the highest student expenses.
- Food and Technology were among the top spending categories.
- Personal care had the lowest spending amount.

## Income Analysis
- Male students generated the highest total monthly income.
- Female and Non-binary students showed similar income patterns.

## Financial Aid Relationship
- Financial aid had a visible relationship with miscellaneous spending.
- Students with higher aid support still maintained significant spending levels.

## Major-Based Spending
- Computer Science students had the highest wellness spending percentage.
- Engineering students recorded the lowest wellness spending percentage.

## Entertainment Trends
- Entertainment spending varied across school years and genders.
- Junior students showed relatively higher entertainment expenses.
